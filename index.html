Section 10

Unpaid Work & the Time Budget

Each spouse reports the hours they personally spend on chores and care, and the hours they think their spouse spends. That gives two independent estimates of the same quantity — what the wife does, and what the husband does. Where the two estimates disagree, someone's contribution is being over- or under-counted at home.

Section 12 · Flagship analysis

Do the Two Spouses Tell the Same Story?

Twenty-odd questions are put to the wife and to the husband separately, about the same facts — did she attend the workshop, how often does she leave the house, who decides. Interviewing both halves of the couple is what makes this possible, and it is the strongest internal validity check the study has. Every row below is one question, scored across the — couples where both interviews are in.
