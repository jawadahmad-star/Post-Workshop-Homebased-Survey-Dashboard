Section 07 · Flagship analysis

Do the Two Spouses Tell the Same Story?

Around thirty questions are put to the wife and to the husband separately, about the same facts — did she attend the workshop, how often does she leave the house, who decides. Interviewing both halves of the couple is what makes this possible, and it is the strongest internal validity check the study has. Every row below is one question, scored across the — couples where both interviews are in.

Agreement Rate by Question

Share of complete couples where the wife and the husband gave the identical answer. Sorted from most contested to most agreed.

Agreement by Question Domain

Question-by-question detail
—
Question Domain Couples Agree Agreement rate Most common disagreement

Agreement is exact-match on the answer, compared on the underlying answer code rather than the printed wording. Several questions are worded from the respondent's own point of view — on who first raised the idea, the same answer reads “I did” to the wife and “Wife” to the husband — so those options are re-labelled to one shared wording before scoring. A couple who agree are never counted as disagreeing merely because the questionnaire phrased their answer differently. Couples where either spouse was not asked the question (because a filter question routed past it) are excluded from that row's denominator, so the couple count varies by question.
